Nockd privacy policy

Applies to the Nockd website and the Nockd iPhone app. Last updated: November 2026.

The short version

Nockd has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (units, colours, theme), the spot you picked for the Day Planner and Forecast, and the last map view are saved in your browser or app storage on your own device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app or clearing the website's data removes them.
  • Your location is only used when you tap "Use my location". It moves the map and forecast to where you are, on your device. Nockd does not send it to us or store it.

The AI coach

If you ask the AI coach a question (Race Planning tab), your question, the recent chat, and a summary of the map you are looking at (region, wind, current, tide, and the race course and marks you placed) are sent to our server and then to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, to write the answer. We do not keep these chats. Anthropic's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy. Please don't type personal information into the coach.

Services that load the maps and data

To show the maps, Nockd loads files from these services. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and basic request information when the files load:

  • Netlify, which hosts Nockd's forecast, tide and current files and the AI coach.
  • EOX (Sentinel-2 cloudless satellite imagery and place-name overlays) and the U.S. Geological Survey (The National Map imagery).
  • Google Fonts and the jsDelivr code library network (website only).
  • Mapbox, only if a Mapbox map style is switched on.

Children

Nockd is made for sailors of all ages, including young sailors with their coaches. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
